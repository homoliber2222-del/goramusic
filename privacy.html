Privacy Policy

GoraMusic: Offline Player

Language: English · Українська

Effective date: October 2026

1. Introduction

GoraMusic: Offline Player (“the App”) is an offline music player. This policy explains what data the App uses. Short version: your music and settings stay on your device; we have no servers and no accounts; the free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores the music files you import, playlists, favorites, play counts, track names and covers you edit, appearance settings, styles and theme images. All of it is stored only locally on your iPhone, we cannot access it, and it is deleted when you delete the App.

3. Importing files

You can add music via the system Files picker, folder import, the Share sheet, Finder file sharing and Wi-Fi transfer. Files go directly into the App on your device. Wi-Fi transfer works only inside your local network and is protected by an optional access code; nothing is sent to the internet or to us.

4. Photos

When you choose an image for a theme background or a track cover, you pick it with the system photo picker. The App receives only that image, without access to your photo library, and the image stays on your device.

5. Backups

Backup files are created only when you ask and are saved where you choose (for example iCloud Drive or Files). We do not have access to them.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt), IP address, approximate location and ad interaction data in order to show and measure ads.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy (in the App) and in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google’s policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

7. In-app purchases

The “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information. Purchases can be restored and shared via Family Sharing.

8. Crash reports

If you enabled sharing with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may provide us anonymous crash reports. We use them only to fix bugs.

9. What we do NOT do

No accounts, no registration, no own servers, no analytics, no selling of data, and no access to your music outside the App.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13; ads are requested as non-personalized where required.

11. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Since we do not store your personal data on our side, there is nothing we can export or delete; all App data can be removed by deleting the App. For ad data, use the consent settings above or contact Google.

12. Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add music?

Open the app and tap Add tracks. There are several ways:

  • Files — pick audio files stored on the iPhone or in a cloud storage.
  • Folder — import a whole folder at once.
  • Wi-Fi transfer — open the shown address on a computer, phone or tablet in the same Wi-Fi network (or scan the QR code), enter the access code and upload the files. Keep the app open while the transfer runs.
  • Share — in another app choose Share and pick GoraMusic.
  • Finder — connect the iPhone to a Mac, open the Files tab and drag the tracks into the app folder.

How do I make a backup?

Settings → Backup → Create backup. Choose a full copy (with the audio files) or a light one (playlists and settings only), then save the file where you want — for example to Files or iCloud Drive. To restore, open Settings → Backup → Restore from backup and pick the file, or simply tap the backup file in the Files app.

How do I change my advertising consent?

In the app open Settings → Privacy and change your choice there. You can also open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn tracking for the app on or off at any time.

Does the app work without the internet?

Yes. Playback, playlists, backups and all settings work offline. The internet is only used to show ads.

Privacy Policy

GoraMusic: Offline Player

Effective date: October 2026

In short: your music and settings stay on your iPhone. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics. The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob.

1. Introduction

GoraMusic: Offline Player (“the App”) is an offline music player for iPhone. This policy explains what data the App uses and how.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores the music files you import, playlists, favorites, play counts, track names and covers you edit, appearance settings, styles and theme images. All of this is stored only locally on your iPhone. We cannot access it.

3. Importing files

You can add music using the system Files picker, folder import, the Share sheet, Finder file sharing and Wi-Fi transfer. Files go directly into the App on your device. Wi-Fi transfer works only inside your local network and can be protected with an access code. Nothing is sent to the internet or to us.

4. Photos

When you choose an image for a theme background or a track cover, you select it with the system photo picker. The App receives only the image you choose, has no access to your photo library, and the image stays on your device.

5. Backups

Backup files are created only when you request them and are saved wherever you choose (for example, iCloud Drive or Files). We have no access to them.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interaction data (for example, ad views and taps).

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App’s Settings → Privacy, and in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google’s policies: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

7. Crash reports

If you have enabled sharing with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. We use them only to fix bugs.

8. What we do not do

No accounts, no registration, no servers of our own, no analytics, no selling of data, and no access to your music outside the App.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data stored by the App stays on your device until you delete it in the App or delete the App. We do not keep copies. Ad-related data is retained by Google according to its privacy policy.

10. Children

The App is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Your rights

We do not store your personal data on our side, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. All App data is removed when you delete the App. For ad-related data, use the consent settings described above or contact Google.

12. Changes to this policy

Any updates will be published on this page with a new effective date.

13. Contact and support
